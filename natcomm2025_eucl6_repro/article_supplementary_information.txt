The low-temperature region between 2 K to 100 K is affected by the contribution of classical Curie 
paramagnetic behavior, due to the high magnetic susceptibility caused by low temperature. The gradual 
decrement in the magnetic susceptibility above 100 K is dominated by Van Vleck paramagnetic behavior, 
with a coupling constant of about 356.76 cm−1, which is within the reported coupling range of some Van 
Vleck paramagnetism about Eu compounds (333~370 cm−1). At room temperature, the total magnetic 
behavior (the sum of Curie paramagnetism and Van Vleck paramagnetism) exhibits a magnetic moment 
of 3.7 μB.
