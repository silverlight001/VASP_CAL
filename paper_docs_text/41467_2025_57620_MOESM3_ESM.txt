Reviewer #3 
(Remarks to the Author) 
The luminescence of traditional chiral organic-inorganic hybrid metal halides is strongly decided by the complicated
coordination environment, thus it is hard to predict their optical properties. In this manuscript, the authors utilized the intrinsic
fingerprint emission of rare-earth ions to break this limitation, and successfully designed and synthesized the novel chiral
europium-based halides, (R/S-3BrMBA)3EuCl6, with predictable and strong red circularly polarized luminescence (CPL).
Particularly, these chiral europium-based halides exhibit both high photoluminescence asymmetry factor and large quantum
yield, which is crucially needed for the construction of high-performance red CPL emitter. Moreover, the degree of
photoluminescence polarization in (R/S-3BrMBA)3EuCl6 can be manipulated by the external magnetic field. Most
importantly, due to the field-generated Zeeman splitting and spin mixing at exciton states, the anomalously positive
magneto-photoluminescence was observed in these chiral europium-based halides. Overall, this manuscript is well-
organized and the conclusion is strongly supported by the experimental results and theoretical calculations. Therefore, this
work is suitable for publication in Nature Communications after the following minor concerns were addressed. 
1.Chiral hybrid perovskites are known to have the Rashba spin-orbit coupling. The authors talked about the chiral-induced
spin-orbit coupling in this work. What is the difference of these two? How does the chiral-induced spin-orbit coupling scale
with the spin selectivity? 
2.Why does the degree of the PL polarization increase with the decreasing of temperature? 
3.Why did the authors choose trivalent europium ions among the 17 rare-earth metal ions? 
4.As shown in Supplementary Table 1, P212121 is a non-centrosymmetric space group, thus the Flack parameter should be
provided. 
5.What is the scanning speed, data pitch and D.I.T in the CD and CPL measurements? The experimental details should be
provided. 
6.The unmarked characteristic transitions of Eu3+ in the PLE spectrum should be provided. 
7.In Supplementary Fig. 7, the coordinate scale of UV-Vis absorption spectra is missing. 
8.In Supplementary Fig. 9, the title of the horizontal axis should be italicized.
